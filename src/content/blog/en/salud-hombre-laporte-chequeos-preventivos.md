Many men only see a doctor when the pain no longer lets them work. The problem is that the most dangerous diseases — high blood pressure, diabetes, high cholesterol, prostate changes — advance silently for years without symptoms. By the time discomfort appears, the damage is often already done. In La Porte, TX you can get ahead of it with a simple preventive checkup, in Spanish and with no appointment.

## Why should men get regular checkups?

- **Catch high blood pressure, diabetes and cholesterol** before they damage your heart, kidneys or vision
- **Monitor your prostate** with the PSA test and detect changes early
- **Know your testosterone level**, which affects energy, mood, sleep and intimacy
- **Find infections that cause no symptoms**, such as some urinary tract or sexually transmitted infections
- **Compare your results year over year** to spot changes before they become a problem

An annual checkup takes less time than a workday and can spare you years of treatment.

## Warning signs you should not ignore

Come in soon if you notice:

- Chest pain or pressure, especially with exertion
- Difficulty urinating, a weak stream, or getting up several times at night
- Excessive thirst, constant hunger or frequent urination
- Frequent headaches or blurry vision
- Constant fatigue or changes in sexual desire
- Lumps or changes in the testicles

## Practical tips to stay healthy between checkups

1. **Move every day**: a 30-minute walk already makes a difference
2. **Watch your plate**: more vegetables and water; less soda, fried food and alcohol
3. **Sleep 7–8 hours**: poor sleep raises blood pressure and blood sugar
4. **Don't smoke**: it's the single change that helps your heart the most
5. **Track your numbers**: blood pressure, weight, glucose — knowing them is the first step
